# M5 game-window follow-up

Read [M5_EXECUTION.md](M5_EXECUTION.md) and the current project handoff.
Use [the full-system Sunday procedure](FULL_SYSTEM_SUNDAY.md) for integrated collection, roster review and acceptance evidence.
This task prepares a real owner-supervised review. It does not grant unattended lineup-write authority.

1. Confirm the intended date and kickoff from the scheduling prompt.
   Follow its schedule-update step before data collection or waiting for the owner.
2. Read `lineup_execution.py status` for incomplete or uncertain actions.
3. Collect current league, ownership, schedule and player evidence through the central clients.
4. Inspect the actual Sleeper team page and native locks.
5. Prepare the current lineup decision and its exact validation findings.
6. Notify the owner that the supervised review is ready.
7. Obtain approval of the concrete proposal and any supported review resolutions.
8. Follow the prepare, dispatch and reconciliation procedure for each necessary change.
9. Record a qualifying game window only after current evidence confirms completion.

If the owner is absent, retain a pending supervised-review status.
Do not invent approval, make an unattended change or count the window as passed.
If no change is needed, validate and confirm that decision without manufacturing a swap.

For a meaningful failure or needed review, use the configured central Pushover notification route.
Use a stable incident ID for this date and window. Do not resend an uncertain incident.
Do not send messages to other managers.

After the early review, retain the same automation with its verified late-review schedule.
Delete that exact automation after the late review through the supported control.
If the game window was missed, record that fact and arrange the next relevant supervised window.
Do not claim M5 complete from simulated tests, a reminder firing or a single game window.
